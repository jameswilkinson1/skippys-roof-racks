## PART 2: Website plan

### 2.1 Principles

- **No shop on the site.** eBay stays the till. The site's jobs are to **win hire bookings**, **send buyers to eBay**, **win fitting and repair jobs**, and **rank for roof box searches**.
- **Very little upkeep.** No product data. eBay buttons are saved searches that update themselves. Phone, hours, review count and hire prices each live in **one settings file**.
- **One main action per page**, with a phone CTA visible all the time.

### 2.2 Tech stack

**Astro** (I'm reading "astra" as Astro; see question 1) as a static site:
- Markdown/MDX content collections for blogs and guides. Pages are fast and need almost no JavaScript, which helps both SEO and Core Web Vitals.
- Built-in image optimisation (AVIF/WebP), sitemap, RSS, and schema markup (JSON-LD) on every page.
- Forms through a hosted form service (Web3Forms, Formspree or Netlify Forms) that sends to info@. No server to maintain.
- Optional **Keystatic or Decap CMS** at `/admin`, so Angela or Isabel can write blog posts without touching code.
- Host on **Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages** (free tier), using the existing domain.
- ⚠️ **Don't touch the email (MX) DNS records** when switching the domain, because info@ runs on it.
- Analytics: GA4 or Plausible, plus Search Console. Track call clicks, eBay outbound clicks and form submits as conversions.

**Technical:**
- Schema: `LocalBusiness`/`AutoPartsStore` with address, coordinates (52.0958, 1.2099), hours, `sameAs` links (eBay, Facebook, Google) and `hasOfferCatalog` for the services. Plus `Service`, `FAQPage`, `Article` and `BreadcrumbList`.
- Unique titles and meta on every page, Open Graph images, XML sitemap, canonicals, fast LCP, alt text on every image.

**Local SEO:** the address shown the same way everywhere (see 1.6), claim the Thule locator listing, a GBP website link that adds UTM tracking, and GBP services that match the site.

**Location pages:** only where we have something real to say. For example "Roof box hire near Colchester" with drive time and collection info. No thin doorway pages.

### 2.7 Blog / Guides plan (first 16 posts)

Built as a **topic cluster**. Every post links up to its pillar page and across to a money page.

**Roof boxes cluster** (pillar: *The UK guide to roof boxes*)

4. **Thule Motion 3 vs Force 3 vs Pulse 2 (vs Motion XT / Force XT).** Which to buy. → used boxes, eBay
5. **Hire or buy a roof box?** A cost comparison, and when buying used beats hiring. → hire + used
6. **Buying a used Thule roof box: a 10-point checklist** (locks, hinges, mounts, scratches, keys). → used hub
7. **Can you fit a roof box without roof rails?** Roof types explained: normal roof, flush rails, raised rails, fixpoints. → fit-my-car
8. **Roof box speed limits, fuel economy and UK rules** (insurance, overhang, roof load limit). → hire
9. **How to fit a Thule roof box (and how to lift it safely)**. Steve demonstrates this in reviews, so a video would work well. → fitting
10. **How to pack a roof box** (what goes up, what stays in, weight limits). → hire
11. **Roof boxes for electric cars** (range, Tesla Model Y and others, roof load). → fit-my-car

**Roof bars cluster**
12. **Which Thule roof bars fit my car? WingBar Evo vs Edge vs ProBar vs SquareBar** → fit-my-car, eBay bars
13. **Thule fitting kits explained (foot packs + kits).** Why the kit number matters. → fit-my-car

**Bike carriers cluster**

**Repairs cluster**

**Every post gets:** a quick answer at the top, tables or images, an FAQ, two contextual links to money pages, a CTA box, and an author box ("Steve Fulcher, Thule specialist since 2012") for E-E-A-T (Google's experience-and-trust signals).

### 2.8 Design direction

- **Clean, bold and practical.** A black and white logo with one strong accent colour (to agree; the green "S" on Facebook suggests green could be the brand colour). It should feel premium and outdoorsy, but **not a copy of Thule's branding**.
- Real photos only: the showroom, the yard (barn and fields), cars, Violet.
- Mobile-first, large tap targets, a sticky call/hire bar, and an accessible type size.
- Reusable blocks: Hero · TrustStrip · ServiceTiles · eBayCategoryGrid · ReviewCarousel · CTAbox · FAQ · HireForm · MapHours.

### 2.9 Build phases

1. **Foundation:** Astro set-up, design system, layout, settings file (contact details, hours, prices, rating), SEO/schema components, redirects.
2. **Core pages:** Home, Hire, Used hub and the 4 category pages, Fitting, Repairs, Fit-my-car, About, Reviews, Contact, legal pages.
