A roof box takes more than most people expect, as long as it's the right kind of stuff. Light, bulky things go up top, and heavy, dense things stay in the car.

Below is what fits in each size of box, worked out from Thule's internal dimensions for the current Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 ranges and the typical sizes of everyday kit.

## What fits in a roof box, by size

| Item | M (390–400 L) | L (440–450 L) | XL (490–500 L) | XXL (590 L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internal length | 164–167 cm | 184–187 cm | 203–207 cm | 220 cm |
| Cabin cases (about 55 x 40 x 20 cm) | 3–4 | 4 | 4–5 | 5 |
| Large cases (about 75–80 cm) | 1–2 | 1–2 | 2 | 2, plus a cabin case or two |
| Folded pushchair | Many compact folds | Yes, most | Yes, with room for bags | Yes, with room for bags |
| Max ski length | 155 cm | 175–180 cm | 200 cm | 215 cm |
| Ski pairs (Thule's figure) | 4–6 | 5–7 | 5–7 | 5–7 |
| Snowboards (Thule's figure) | 3–4 | 3–5 | 3–5 | 3–5 |
| Golf bags | 1–2 | 1–2 | 2 | 2–3 |
| Camping or festival kit | Tent, sleeping bags and mats for two | A small family's soft kit | Family tent, bedding and chairs | A big family's kit for a week |

The ski length, ski pair and snowboard figures come straight from Thule. The rest are rough guides based on the internal dimensions and typical item sizes, not guarantees. Boxes taper at the nose and tail and the lid curves down at the sides, so if it's close, measure your actual kit.

## Suitcases

Suitcases fit, but they're not the most efficient thing to put in a roof box. Hard cases are rigid rectangles and roof boxes aren't, so soft holdalls and duffel bags usually fill the space better.

Height is the main limit. Standard Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 boxes are around 37–39 cm tall inside at the highest point, so cases go in flat, in a single layer. The shallower Low boxes (29–33 cm inside) are better for skis and soft bags than chunky cases. For a full breakdown by litre size, see our guide to [how many suitcases fit in a roof box](/guides/how-many-suitcases-fit-in-a-roof-box/).

## Pushchairs

A folded pushchair is one of the best things to put in a roof box: bulky, awkward in the boot and fairly light. Thule itself names its L and XL boxes as the sizes for strollers.

As an example, Thule's own Urban Glide 3 folds to 88 x 58 x 31 cm, which sits within the floor of even a medium box (around 165 x 69 cm inside) and under the lid height. Bulkier travel systems with a carrycot or a wide chassis may need an L or XL.

Check the folded size in your pushchair's manual against the box's internal length, width and height, and remember the pushchair's weight counts towards your roof load. If you're after a pushchair that folds neatly, we also sell [Thule pushchairs, dog crates and more](/more-thule/).

## Skis and snowboards by length

Ski length is the one measurement you can't fudge. If your skis are longer than the box's rated maximum, the lid won't close properly, so check the length (usually printed on the ski) before you choose.

| Max ski length | Current Thule boxes |
|---|---|
| 155 cm (5 ft 1 in) | Motion 3 M, Force 3 M, Pulse 2 M |
| 175 cm (5 ft 9 in) | Force 3 L, Pulse 2 L, Force 3 L Sport, Motion 3 Sport |
| 180 cm (5 ft 11 in) | Motion 3 L |
| 200 cm (6 ft 7 in) | Motion 3 XL and XL Low, Force 3 XL, Pulse 2 XL |
| 215 cm (7 ft 1 in) | Motion 3 XXL and XXL Low, Force 3 XXL Sport |

If you're looking at an older box, Thule's internal dimensions guide gives 180 cm for the Motion XT L and Force XT L, 200 cm for the XT XLs, and 215 cm for the Motion XT XXL and both XT Alpine models.

Poles fit alongside the skis without trouble. The narrow Sport boxes mount to one side of the bars, which leaves room for a bike or kayak carrier next to them.

## Golf bags

A typical golf bag is roughly 86–91 cm tall empty and around 125 cm long with a driver in it. Every box from M upwards is long enough, since they're all 164 cm or more inside.

Width decides how many. Two slim carry bags should sit side by side in most medium and large boxes, while bulkier tour or cart bags need the extra width of an XL or XXL. Clubs are dense and heavy for their size, so keep an eye on the total weight if you're carrying more than one bag.

## Camping and festival kit

This is where a roof box really earns its place. Tents, sleeping bags, roll mats, pillows, folding chairs and windbreaks are all light for their size, and getting them out of the car frees up space for people.

- **Up top:** tent, sleeping bags, mats, pillows, chairs, coats and spare clothes.
- **In the car:** cool boxes, water carriers, food, cooking kit and anything that could leak.
- **Long items:** folding chairs and tent poles are fine in any box long enough to take them flat.

For a festival, a medium box usually takes the soft kit for two people. A family camping for a week will make good use of an XL or XXL.

## What not to put in a roof box

A roof box isn't a second boot. Some things are better kept inside the car:

- **Heavy, dense items** such as drinks, tins, tools or a full cool box.
- **Anything that makes the lid strain to close.** If you have to lean on it, take something out.
- **Anything sticking out.** The Highway Code says your load must be secure and must not stick out dangerously.
- **More weight than the lower of two limits:** the box's 75 kg rating, or your car's maximum roof load minus the weight of the box and bars.

<Callout type="warning" title="Check your roof load">Your car's maximum roof load is in the vehicle handbook, and it includes the roof bars and the box itself. Current Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 boxes weigh roughly 15 to 25 kg empty, so work out what's left before you pack.</Callout>
