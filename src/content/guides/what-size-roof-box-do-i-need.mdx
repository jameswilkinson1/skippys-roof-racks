The right roof box size comes down to three things: how much you're carrying, the longest item you need to fit in, and the size of your car. As a rough guide, a couple manages well with a medium box of around 400 litres, while a family of four is usually happiest with an XL of around 500 litres.

Below we've pulled together Thule's own UK figures for the current Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 ranges, so you can match a size to your trip and your car.

## Roof box sizes at a glance

Thule sells its boxes by size class (S, M, L, XL, XXL) rather than by litres, and the exact volume varies a little between ranges. Thule says XL and XXL are the most common sizes, which makes sense, as they suit a family holiday.

| Size | Volume (current Thule range) | Max ski length | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 300 L (Force 3 S) | Not designed for skis | Shorter cars and everyday bags |
| Sport (narrow) | 300–450 L | 175–215 cm | Skis and slim loads, leaving room on the bars for a bike |
| M | 390–400 L | 155 cm (5 ft 1 in) | Couples, or a small family on a short trip |
| L | 440–450 L | 175–180 cm | Smaller families and keen skiers |
| XL | 490–500 L | 200 cm (6 ft 7 in) | A family of four on a week away |
| XXL | 590 L | 215 cm (7 ft 1 in) | Bigger families, long trips and long skis |

The "Low" versions of the Motion 3 hold less (370 to 480 litres) because they're shallower. They keep the same footprint, though, so the XL Low and XXL Low take the same ski lengths as the full-height XL and XXL.

## What the litres actually mean

A litre is 1,000 cubic centimetres, so a 500 litre box holds half a cubic metre. That sounds huge, but a roof box is long, wide and shallow, with a tapered nose and a curved lid. You can't fill every corner the way you can a square boot.

That's why two boxes with similar litres can carry quite different loads. The Motion 3 XL Low holds 400 litres in the same footprint as the 490 litre Motion 3 XL, but its internal height drops from 37 cm to 30 cm. It's great for skis and soft bags, less so for a chunky hard suitcase.

Thule measures internal dimensions at the longest, widest and tallest usable point, so treat them as maximums. Beyond the litres, check:

- **Internal length** if you carry skis, snowboards, golf bags or fishing rods.
- **Internal height** if you carry hard suitcases or a folded pushchair. Standard Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 boxes are around 37–39 cm tall inside at the highest point, a little less in the Force 3 S and Force 3 L Sport.
- **Max load.** Every current Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 box is rated to carry 75 kg.

## Size by who's travelling

- **Couple, weekend away:** 300–400 litres. An M box or the compact Force 3 S takes a couple of soft holdalls, coats and walking boots.
- **Family of three or four, a week away:** 450–500 litres. An L or XL swallows the bulky, light stuff (bedding, coats, a folded pushchair) and frees up the boot for heavier bags.
- **Bigger families, camping or two weeks away:** 590 litres. The Motion 3 XXL is the largest box in Thule's current UK range.
- **Ski trips:** size by ski length first, then check the volume works for everything else.

## Size by car

A bigger box isn't automatically better. It has to suit your roof as well as your luggage.

**Length and the tailgate.** The box mustn't stop your boot opening. Thule calls the minimum clearance "R-min": measure from the centre of your front roof bar to the point where the open tailgate meets the roof, then compare it with the figure in the box's manual. As a very rough guide, hatchbacks and smaller estates often suit an M (175 cm long) or the stubby Force 3 S (140 cm), while big estates, MPVs and SUVs can usually take an XL (215 cm) or XXL (232 cm).

**Overhang.** A little overhang at the front is fine, but the box shouldn't get in the way of your view out of the windscreen. Too far forward also means more wind noise and drag.

**Height.** Standard Motion 3 boxes sit 38–40 cm above the bar, while the Low versions sit 30–32 cm above it. That matters if you park in a garage or use height-restricted car parks, so measure the total height of car, bars and box before you need to know it.

## Don't forget the weight

Litres tell you how much fits; your car's roof load limit tells you how much you can carry. You'll find the maximum roof load in your vehicle handbook, and it includes the weight of the bars and the box itself, not just what's inside.

Current Motion 3, Force 3 and Pulse 2 boxes weigh roughly 15 to 25 kg empty, so on a car with a modest roof limit, the box and bars can use up a good chunk of it before you've packed a thing. Check the handbook, check the box's manual, and keep heavy items in the car. The Highway Code says you must not overload your vehicle and your load must be secure.
